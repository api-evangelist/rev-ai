The article explores six major learning styles - visual, auditory, reading/writing, kinesthetic, logical/analytical, and social/linguistic - and provides strategies for teachers to accommodate diverse learners in the classroom. It emphasizes that while learning styles remain scientifically debated, teachers widely use them to personalize instruction, and discusses how transcription technology can support multiple learning preferences simultaneously.
